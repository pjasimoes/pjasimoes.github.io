Short Bio

Paulo Simões is a heliophysicist with 20+ years of experience in solar flare studies using multi-wavelength data, from radio waves to gamma rays, and simulations. Since 2019, he is a professor at the Engineering School of the Mackenzie Presbyterian University (UPM, Brazil) and researcher at the Center for Radio Astronomy & Astrophysics Mackenzie (CRAAM). Obtained his MSc in Astrophysics in 2005 and PhD in Space Geophysics in 2009 at the National Institute for Space Research (INPE, Brazil), and was a post-doctoral research assistant in the Astronomy & Astrophysics group of the University of Glasgow (UK) from 2011 to 2018, working on the European projects F-CHROMA and HESPE.
